Tic Tac Cube

Tic-tac-toe, but in three dimensions. Get a full row — across, down, through, or corner to corner — on a 3×3×3, 4×4×4 or 5×5×5 cube.

Games start from the iPhone app: a friend sends you a link, you open it here and play. No account needed to watch; sign in with your Apple ID to make moves.
