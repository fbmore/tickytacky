Across a layer, straight down, or corner to corner through the whole cube. Spin it, spread the layers apart, and see the line before they do.

Word Cube

Words that run through the layers.

Trace from letter to touching letter: up, down, left, right, forward or back. Find enough words and the rest of the cube reveals itself.

Cube Pop

Gravity follows your hand.

Trace three or more touching orbs of one colour and pop them. Turn the cube to choose which way the rest fall, and set up the next chain.

  • Long chains make bombs, beams and wild prisms
  • Gold orbs double your points
  • Today’s challenge, 30-move games and endless Zen
