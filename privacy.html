Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

TickyTacky is made by FRANCESCO BERTOCCI, LLC. We built it to collect as little as possible: there are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. This page explains exactly what the app and the web player store, where it lives, and who can see it.

In short
  • Solo play, settings and progress stay in your iCloud and on your device. We can’t see them.
  • Games with friends are stored in Apple’s iCloud, in a shared game database. Each move carries your chosen display name and an anonymous iCloud ID.
  • Anyone with a game’s link can see that game, including the names in it. We can see these game records too.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking, location, contacts, photos, microphone, email address or payment details. Ever.

What’s stored, and where

On your device and in your own iCloud

The iPhone and iPad app keeps these in Apple’s iCloud key-value storage (which syncs between your own devices) and in the app’s local settings:

  • Your display name, the second player’s name for same-device games, and your piece colours.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, threat hints, piece style, board sizes, computer level.
  • Games in progress against the computer or on the same device, Word Cube progress, Cube Pop boards and best scores.
  • A list of your games with friends: the game ID, your opponent’s display name and when you last opened it.

Only you can read this data. It never reaches us.

Games with friends (Apple iCloud, shared game database)

When you start or join a game with a friend, each action is saved as a small record in the public database of the iCloud container iCloud.com.fbmore.TicTacCube, run by Apple. A record holds:

FieldWhat it is
Game IDThe random code in the game’s link
Kind, round, cell, timeWhat happened (join, move, word, pop, message, rematch, resign, end) and when
TextYour display name when you join, a preset quick message, the word you played, or the orbs you popped
AuthorYour iCloud user record ID for this app: an anonymous identifier, not your Apple Account email or name
Display name and colourAs you chose them in Settings

Who can see these records: the players of that game; anyone who has the game’s link (links are not secret: treat them like an invitation); and FRANCESCO BERTOCCI, LLC, through Apple’s CloudKit tools, to run and support the service. Messages are limited to a fixed set of preset phrases; there is no free-text chat.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app asks iCloud to alert you when it’s your turn or a friend sends a quick message (an iCloud subscription per game), and schedules one local reminder on your device 24 hours after it became your turn. Notifications are optional and can be turned off in Settings → Notifications → TickyTacky.

The web player

The browser version at fbmore.github.io/tictaccube stores the same kinds of settings and progress in your browser’s local storage, on your device only. To play a game with a friend in the browser you sign in with Apple through Apple’s CloudKit JS; the records it writes are the same as above.

Device permissions

The only permission TickyTacky asks for is notifications, and only when you start or open a game with a friend (or turn it on in Settings). It does not use your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, motion sensors or Bluetooth.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, advertising IDs or tracking across apps and websites.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • No email address, phone number, real name (unless you choose to use it as your display name), location, contacts, photos or audio.
  • No payment information: the app is free with no in-app purchases.

Third-party services

  • Apple: iCloud and CloudKit store your private settings and the shared game records; Sign in with Apple is used by the web player. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple’s handling of this data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the web player. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when you load the pages.

Keeping and deleting data

Settings and progress in your iCloud stay until you delete the app’s data (iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage) or remove the app. Records of games with friends are kept until they’re deleted. To have records of your games deleted, email fbmore+tictaccube@gmail.com with the game links or IDs; we’ll remove them, usually within 30 days. Ending a game from the app stops it for both players.

Children

TickyTacky is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players choose a display name that others in their games can see; parents may wish to suggest a nickname. If you believe a child has shared personal information in a display name, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
