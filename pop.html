Cube Pop

Trace 3 or more touching orbs of one colour and pop them. Turn the cube to choose which way the rest fall.

Specials

  • 💣Pop 5 in one chain — makes a bomb: pops its 6 neighbours when used.
  • ✳️Pop 6 — makes a beam: clears its whole line in the direction things fall.
  • 🔮Pop 7 or more — makes a prism: wild, joins a chain of any colour.
  • ✨Gold orbs — double the points of the chain they’re in.
  • ↯Chain reactions — specials caught in a blast go off too, multiplying the score.

Scoring

  • ★Chain length² × 10, +15 for each extra orb a blast takes, ×1.5 for every special that goes off.
  • ⇣“Down” is whichever face of the cube points to the bottom of your screen — a soft glow marks it.
  • ⇄Two players take turns on the same cube, ten pops each. Highest score wins.
