    How to play

    • ☝︎Tap a letter, then a neighbouring one. The rings show where you can go next.
    • ↶Tap the last letter again to take it back.
    • ⬡Letters connect through faces only: no diagonals. Each letter once per word.
    • ✦Find words to reveal the rest of the cube, in three waves.
    • ★Longer words score more. A word through every layer earns +3.
    • ✓Other real words are accepted as extras: they’re kept with your words but don’t score.
    • ⌨︎On a keyboard: Enter submits, Backspace takes a letter back, Esc clears.
